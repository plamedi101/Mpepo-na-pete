Refund policy

Until mid‑December 2026, the service is free: there is nothing to refund. After that, you pay €2 to have a check-in carried out; if it is not carried out, you are refunded.

This page is a courtesy translation of the French original. If there is any difference, the French version prevails. Read the French original.

In brief

  • Check-in not carried out by us: refund of your €2.
  • Check-in carried out and boarding pass sent: no refund.
  • No refund either if the failure comes from incorrect information or an invalid document that you provided.
  • Refund no later than 14 days after your request, by the same means as your payment.

When you are refunded

We were unable to carry out the check-in
Refund of your €2, whatever the reason: refusal by the airline, changed flight, airline website unavailable, request arrived too late. The only exception: the cases listed below.
You cancel before the check-in
Refund of your €2, as long as the check-in has not been carried out.
You paid twice, or too much
We refund you the difference.

When you are not refunded

The check-in is carried out
The service is delivered as soon as your boarding pass is sent to you. If your flight is then changed or cancelled, or if you miss it, contact your airline: it no longer depends on FlyFast.
The error comes from the information provided
Incorrect or incomplete information, expired passport, invalid document: we carried out the process, but it could not succeed.

How to request a refund

Write to us on WhatsApp at +20 150 831 9385, giving your request number (for example FF-AB12CD) and the reason for your request. If it is justified, we refund you no later than 14 days after your request.

The refund is made by the same means as your payment, unless we agree otherwise with you. We refund the €2 paid; any fees charged by your bank or provider, for example for currency exchange, are not our responsibility.

Right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer in the European Union, you also have a 14 day right of withdrawal, with an exception once the service has been fully performed. It is explained in section 9 of the terms of sale.
