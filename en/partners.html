Our travel partners

Before, during and after a flight, other services can come in handy: internet on arrival, tickets, transfers, a rental car… Here are the ones we offer.

Good to know before you click

  • These are partner links: if you book or buy after a click, FlyFast may receive a commission, at no extra cost to you.
  • Each link carries the label “partner link”.
  • The order, the payment and the after-sales service are handled by the partner, at its own prices and under its own terms. FlyFast does not control them.
  • Your FlyFast check-in request does not depend on these services, and your travel information is never passed to them. What happens when you click.

eSIM and travel SIM

To have internet from the moment you land, with no roaming fees.

Check that the plan covers your destination before you buy.

  • Airalopartner link

    Travel eSIM shop: data plans by country, by region or worldwide, to install before you leave.

  • Sailypartner link

    Travel eSIM launched by Nord Security, the company behind NordVPN: data plans by country or by region.

  • Yesimpartner link

    Travel eSIM with data plans by country, by region or for several destinations.

  • Drimsimpartner link

    Prepaid travel SIM card: one card for several countries, with a balance you top up.

  • GigSkypartner link

    Travel eSIM: data plans for many destinations, to buy and activate online.

Flights

To compare prices and times before you book.

The ticket is sold by the site you choose: FlyFast plays no part in this booking.

  • Aviasalespartner link

    Flight search engine: compares offers from many airlines and online travel agencies.

  • Kiwi.compartner link

    Online travel agency that compares flights and can combine several airlines on a single trip.

Compensation for a delayed or cancelled flight

To claim compensation after a flight that was badly delayed, cancelled or overbooked.

You can also claim it yourself, free of charge, from the airline. These services do it for you in exchange for a commission: read their terms before you commit.

  • AirHelppartner link

    Compensation claims for a delayed, cancelled or overbooked flight: the service reviews your case and follows it up for you.

  • Compensairpartner link

    It checks whether you are eligible, then claims from the airline for a delayed, cancelled or overbooked flight.

Luggage storage

To drop your bags for a few hours, between the hotel and your flight time.

  • Radical Storagepartner link

    Network of luggage storage points in shops and hotels, bookable online in many cities.

Transfers

To book a driver in advance between the airport, the station and your accommodation.

Check the final price and the cancellation terms before you pay.

  • Kiwitaxipartner link

    Transfers booked in advance to and from airports, in many countries.

  • Welcome Pickupspartner link

    Transfers with a driver from airports and ports, in many countries.

  • GetTransfer.compartner link

    Transfer marketplace: you describe your trip and drivers send you their offers.

  • intui.travelpartner link

    Booking of transfers with a driver, notably between airports, stations and accommodation.

Car rental

To compare rental companies before you book.

Check the deposit, the mileage, the insurance and the driver’s minimum age.

  • Auto Europepartner link

    Booking of rental cars from several rental companies.

  • Economybookings.compartner link

    Rental car comparison site, with offers from several rental companies.

  • GetRentacar.compartner link

    Booking of rental cars in many countries.

  • Localrent.compartner link

    Marketplace of local rental companies: online booking with independent companies in the country you visit.

  • QEEQpartner link

    Rental of cars and motorhomes.
