How it works

A simple process, with clear terms.

  1. You send your details

    Booking reference (PNR), full name, passport and flight details, using the form below.

  2. You pay €2 per booking

    We confirm the payment arrangements with you on WhatsApp. Card payment through Stripe will be available soon.

  3. We check in your flight

    Our team completes the process on your airline’s official website.

  4. You receive your boarding pass

    Straight to WhatsApp, ready to print or show.

Important: your request must reach us no later than 10 hours before take-off. After that, we cannot guarantee that the check-in will be done in time: in that case, go straight to your airline’s check-in desk at the airport.

Please read before sending

FlyFast is an independent assistance service, not affiliated with your airline. Our team completes your check-in directly on the airline’s official website.

We cannot guarantee the check-in if the airline refuses it, if the flight is changed, if a document is invalid, or if your request reaches us less than 10 hours before departure.

Your information is encrypted as soon as it is sent and is used only for your check-in. It is then erased: as soon as the check-in is done, and at the latest 14 days after your request. Only an empty record remains, with no personal data. See the details

By sending your request, you ask us to carry out the check-in without delay. If you are a consumer in the European Union, you can withdraw until the check-in is done, but not afterwards. If we do not carry out the check-in, you are refunded, unless there is an error in the information provided. See the terms of sale and the refund policy.

Frequently asked questions

Another question? Write to us on WhatsApp.

What happens if I send my request less than 10 hours before the flight?

We cannot guarantee that the check-in will be done in time. In that case, go straight to your airline’s check-in desk at the airport.

Is this service affiliated with my airline?

No. FlyFast is an independent assistance service. We use your airline’s official website to complete your check-in, just as any passenger would.

Is my data (passport, etc.) safe?

It is encrypted as soon as it is sent, used only to complete your check-in, then erased: as soon as it is done, and at the latest 14 days after your request. Only an empty record remains, with no personal data. Read the Privacy page.

What happens if the airline refuses the check-in?

This can happen for reasons beyond our control (invalid document, changed flight, specific restriction). We cannot guarantee the outcome of the check-in, only the process carried out on your behalf. If we are unable to complete it, you are refunded your €2, unless the refusal is due to incorrect information or an invalid document. Read the refund policy.

How do I pay the €2?

€2 per booking, whatever the number of passengers. For now, the payment arrangements are confirmed to you on WhatsApp once we have received your request. Card payment, with Stripe, will be available soon.

There are several of us on the same booking: what should I do?

Fill in the form once for each passenger, with the same booking reference (PNR). You pay only €2 for the whole booking.
